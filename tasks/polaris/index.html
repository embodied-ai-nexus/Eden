    PolaRiS¶

    Eden ports the six DROID scenes of PolaRiS, a real-to-sim evaluation suite in which a Franka with a Robotiq 2F-85 works in front of a Gaussian-splat capture of a real scene. Read this page to run the scenes, score a policy with PolaRiS's rubrics, or generate demonstrations. The code is in eden/tasks/polaris/.

    The tasks¶

    Each scene is registered twice: a single-env task for teleoperation and policy inference, and a *_batched variant that renders many envs at once.

    Task (--task) Batched variant Instruction Rubric (success)
    polaris_block_stack_kitchen polaris_block_stack_kitchen_batched "Place and stack the blocks on top of the green tray" both cubes put in the tray, then the green cube over the wood cube
    polaris_food_bussing polaris_food_bussing_batched "Put all the foods in the bowl" the ice cream and the grapes put in the bowl
    polaris_pan_clean polaris_pan_clean_batched "Use the yellow sponge to scrub the blue handle frying pan" the sponge put in the pan
    polaris_move_latte_cup polaris_move_latte_cup_batched "put the latte art cup on top of the cutting board" the cup put on the cutting board
    polaris_organize_tools polaris_organize_tools_batched "put the scissor into the large container" the scissor put in the large container
    polaris_tape_into_container polaris_tape_into_container_batched "put the tape into the container" the tape put in the container
    • Single-env tasks are EdenSplatConfigs (built as a SplatEnvBase): one env, no rewards or terminations beyond the episode length, for teleoperation and policy inference.
    • Batched tasks are EdenSplatRLConfigs (built as a SplatRLEnvBase): four envs by default, the gs-madrona batch renderer with a soft two-light rig, and every env's splat rendered and composited under it. They reuse the single-env task's objects, splat, instruction and rubric verbatim. Their reward and termination managers are empty for now: they are RL-typed scaffolding that renders batched but has nothing to train against yet. env_options.num_envs and splat_options.minibatch trade VRAM for throughput.

    Scoring: PolaRiS's rubrics¶

    PolaRiS scores each task with a rubric: criteria built from three checkers, each counted once it has ever passed, some only after others have. Success is every criterion reached; progress is the fraction reached. Every Polaris task, batched or not, carries one success metric, rubric (a RubricMetricTerm), built in eden/tasks/polaris/rubric.py:

    PolaRiS checker Eden criterion
    reach(obj, 0.2) EeNearEntity: the gripper's base link (PolaRiS's ee_frame, the Robotiq base_link) within 20 cm of the object
    lift(obj, h) object_bottom_above_height: the object's lowest point h above the table
    is_within_xy(a, b, fraction) the gripper open (left_driver_joint < 0.1 rad, PolaRiS's finger_joint test) and object_a_within_b_xy

    Most tasks are one put_in(object, container) chain: reach, lift, then let go over the container (80 % of the object's footprint over the container's).

    Lift is measured from the table, not from the spawn height

    PolaRiS compares the object's origin with its spawn height. The scanned assets put their origins anywhere, and Eden drops its objects onto the table at reset, so the height is measured from the table top (z = 0 in every scene) to the object's lowest point instead.

    The robot and the scene¶

    • Robot. PolarisDroid: a gravity-compensated Franka arm and a Robotiq 2F-85, welded at the arm's attachment link. It differs from Eden's Droid in its home pose (calibrated to the splat cameras) and in a 2 cm torsional friction patch on the pads, without which a pair of scissors pinched across its middle pivots and slips out once lifted. The gripper is the pad-only DROID gripper: a full-collision gripper penetrates mesh objects and ejects them on contact.
    • Physics. sim_dt=0.004 with 4 substeps, so the substep is 1 ms and the 2F-85's closed loop stays stiff, and torsional friction on. Graspable objects are stiffened per object (default_geoms_sol_params), never through a global constraint time constant, which ejects mesh objects on contact.
    • Control. decimation=5: 50 Hz, a DifferentialIKController on attachment (ee_controller) and a binary gripper on the two driver joints (gripper_controller). The episode is 30 s.
    • Cameras. external_camera and wrist_camera, with intrinsics written for a 1280 × 720 image, each compositing the splat behind the rendered scene (SplatComposite, the ground plane drawn by the splat).
    • Splats and objects. Each scene's splat (<scene>/assets/.../splat.ply) and its objects (eden.options.objects.polaris) download on first use from the eden_polaris dataset into polaris/ under the asset directory.

    Requirements¶

    Splat rendering goes through gsplat, which JIT-compiles a CUDA extension on first import, so the CUDA toolkit (nvcc) must be installed.

    uv sync --inexact --extra vis           # single-env tasks
uv sync --inexact --extra batch-splat   # batched tasks

    pip install 'eden-world[vis]'           # single-env tasks
pip install 'eden-world[batch-splat]'   # batched tasks

    The batched tasks also need gs-madrona, the CUDA-only batch renderer, installed separately. See Installation and Sensors and rendering.

    Evaluating a DROID policy¶

    The DROID-family remote-policy clients (PiPolicy with policy_setup="droid" or "droid_jointpos", Gr00tPolicy with "oxe_droid_relative_eef_relative_joint", Cosmos3Policy, MolmoAct2Policy and GalaxeaVlaPolicy with "droid_franka") target this rig. Replace the config's observation and action options with the client's, as on the SIMPLER page.

    Set the env to DROID's 15 Hz first

    Every DROID checkpoint's action row is one 1/15 s step (DROID's RobotEnv.control_hz), and the DROID clients check the env's control rate when they are constructed. Eden's PolaRiS tasks step every 20 ms (50 Hz), which would replay each chunk 3.3× too fast. Set the timing PolaRiS itself uses (droid_cfg.py: sim.dt = 1/120, decimation = 8), with sim_substeps=9 to keep the Robotiq's substep under 1 ms:

    cfg.env_options.sim_dt = 1 / 120
cfg.env_options.decimation = 8
cfg.env_options.sim_substeps = 9

    See Remote policies for the DROID setups and their checks.

    Scripted demonstrations¶

    Every *_batched task has an eden datagen demonstrator (eden/tasks/polaris/scripted_demos.py): the put-on tool routine on the DROID rig, one put-on per object the rubric scores, reading object footprints off the collision meshes (privileged). They are registered on the batched tasks because eden datagen needs episode ends and many envs, which the single-env tasks do not have.

    eden datagen --task polaris_move_latte_cup_batched --num_envs 16 --num_episodes 64

    What each demonstrator does and its success rates are in Scripted demonstrations.
