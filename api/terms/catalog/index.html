    Term Catalog¶

    Every term registered in Eden (246 of them) with the parameters it accepts, generated from the registries themselves. This is the complete list; the guide's Terms & Registries page explains how to write and wire one up.

    • Bold parameters are required — omit one and configure() raises before the env is built.
    • A term's parameters are the keys of its params={...} dict (function terms) or its configure() fields (class terms). The fields every term of a kind carries are listed once, under that kind.
    • A Requires column appears in the tables that have one: an EnvOptions flag the term needs set, which no parameter of the term can supply. The env build raises without it.
    • eden terms prints the same catalog in a terminal, eden terms --kind reward one section of it, eden terms --grep height only the terms whose name, parameters or summary match, and eden terms --json emits it as JSON.
